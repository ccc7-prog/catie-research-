From the redesigned document

The ice gets older as you move deeper into the ice core. This is because new layers form when snow falls and compacts over time, creating the youngest ice at the top. As you move further down, you find older ice that was buried and compacted long ago.

CO2 levels have fluctuated throughout Earth's history. In the graph, CO2 levels increased with depth when they ranged between 750 and 1900 ppm. However, they continued to increase in ppm as the depth decreased after this interval, so there was no real pattern. The CO2 levels were always increasing.

​ When looking at the graph I found that the maximum temperature occurred 133.01 thousand(s) years ago when the temperature was -52.33 C. The minimum temperature occurred 19.83 thousand(s) years ago when the temperature was -65.272 C.
