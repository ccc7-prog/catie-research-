From the redesigned document

We tested the water speed by placing a feather in three different widths of our section of the creek and timing how long it took to travel 5 meters from the starting point. Each time recorded represents the number of seconds it took for the feather to travel the 5-meter distance.

Lobos Creek and ground water produced around 950 gallons per minute that day, supplying 295 gallons per minute to each of the two pipes, with 360 gallons per minute flowing to Baker Beach.

To calculate the percent error of our measurement of the creek's flow rate, we used the accurate value measured by the Presidio Treatment Plant and plugged it into the percent error formula.
