About this work

Developed a proposal for community centers offering mentorship, tutoring, career pathways, and supportive services to help reduce foster-youth involvement in the criminal legal system.

A legislative and community-program proposal, not an implemented program. The original student work is preserved below, including its original wording and any limitations.

From the redesigned document

1. PROPOSAL SUMMARY: Constructing community centers to support children in foster care and those in low-income communities, with the hope of averting their entry into the foster care-to-prison pipeline. The construction of these community centers will involve individuals who have experienced the foster-to-prison pipeline, enabling them to leverage their experiences.

Proposed community center resources ●​ Open dialogue therapy ●​ Food ●​ Pool ●​ Ways to access club Sports teams and music/ art opportunities ●​ Essay scholarships for college ●​ Tutoring ●​ Emphasis on nature ●​ Cultural connection - dances/music/food ●​ Previous convicts advise - open dialogue ●​ Job opportunities with a clear career path ○​ Example: Lawyer ■​ There would be opportunities to apply for paid internships at law firms, with the possibility of earning scholarships for law school based on performance. Establishing a clear future plan can help deter the notion that joining a gang is the sole path to financial stability and serve as a reminder of their potential future.
